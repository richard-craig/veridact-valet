# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. Everyone who takes part in the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
meetings and mailing lists) is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, criticise ideas rather than people, and remember that contributors
work for different organisations, including competitors. Disagreement about design is welcome; harassment and
personal attacks are not.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@veridact.net**. Reports are handled confidentially by the
maintainers. A maintainer who is the subject of a report takes no part in handling it.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
